Meri favourite person ke naam, ♡

Pata hai, kabhi-kabhi main sochta hoon ki aapko kaise bataun ki aap mere liye kitni special hain. Main saamne hota hoon toh har baat sahi se bol nahi paata, isliye aaj thoda likh kar keh raha hoon.

Mujhe aapki badi-badi baaton se hi nahi, aapki chhoti-chhoti aadaton se bhi pyaar hai. Coaching mein aapka meri dictation ki galti pakadna, phir woh chhoti si smile… aur aapka mere saath bahar aate waqt bye kehna — ye sab shayad aapke liye normal moments hon, par mere liye bahut pyaare hain.

Aap paas hoti hain toh normal sa din bhi thoda achha lagne lagta hai. Kabhi aapki smile yaad aa jaati hai, kabhi aapki koi baat, aur bina kisi special reason ke bhi face par smile aa jaati hai. Shayad pyaar aisi hi chhoti-chhoti cheezon mein bhi hota hai.

Main ye nahi kehta ki main hamesha perfect rahunga. Kabhi mujhse galti hoti hai, kabhi main apni baat sahi se samjha nahi paata. Lekin main chahta hoon ki main aapko aur achhe se samajhna seekhun, aapki baat dhyaan se sunun, aur apne actions se bhi dikhau ki aap mere liye kitni important hain.

Aapko har waqt kuch prove karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Aap jaisi hain, apni hasi, apni baaton aur apne little quirks ke saath, mere liye bahut pyaari hain. Main bas chahta hoon ki hum saath mein aur bhi aise moments banayein jinhe baad mein yaad karke hum dono smile karein.

Thank you meri life mein aane ke liye, aur meri ordinary si days ko apne tareeke se special banane ke liye. Main aapse bahut pyaar karta hoon — shayad main har baar perfect words mein na keh paun, par ye feeling mere liye bahut real hai. ❤️

Bahut saare pyaar ke saath,
Aapka person ♥

Aur ek chhoti si baat… 🤭

Aapke liye, dil se banaya. ♡
